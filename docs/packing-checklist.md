# Packing Checklist

The night-before list, printable and checkable. Two totes, one
wagon, one box — everything that leaves the house for an event,
divided by job. The lists are designed around the
[two-tote load-in](03-booth-setup-on-a-budget.md): **Tote 1 is the
structure** (canopy, table, chair, weights), **Tote 2 is the
contents** (cloth, product, signs, money kit), and both lids get a
printed copy of this page.

## Tote 1: Structure (goes up first)

- [ ] Canopy, with its stakes/pegs bagged *to the frame*
- [ ] Canopy weights — all four, bungee'd to the frame for carry
- [ ] Sidewalls (1-2, weather season only)
- [ ] Table (or table rental receipt if renting; check the
      organizer's [table answer](01-finding-events.md))
- [ ] Tablecloth + skirt cloth
- [ ] Chair (one is enough; you stand more than you sit)
- [ ] Riser crates (2), the flat cart or suitcase shelving
- [ ] Hand cart / wagon
- [ ] Bungee cords (4), duct tape lap, sharpie

## Tote 2: Contents (the product layer)

- [ ] Stock by line, packed [by the bring-formula](07-restock-math.md),
      counted, in order of table placement
- [ ] Van-box: the [one back-stock box](07-restock-math.md) for
      hero lines (one per event, labeled)
- [ ] Price cards / tags / the [three-price raft](04-pricing-and-making-change.md) signs
- [ ] Banner / tabletop sign / business cards or tags
- [ ] Display props in their labeled pouch (frames, stands, mirror)
- [ ] Bags for customers (small paper or reusable, stocked)
- [ ] Tissue / ribbon / small-gift wrap (if you offer it)

## The money kit (the box with the lid card)

Everything money lives in one closable box with a card taped in the
lid:

- [ ] **The lid card**, printed:
      starting float breakdown ($40 ones / $25 fives / $20 tens /
      $10 coins = **$85**)
- [ ] Cash box *or* money apron (make-change happens [at the body](04-pricing-and-making-change.md))
- [ ] Card reader, charged, with its cable
- [ ] Phone, charged — *and* phone charger + battery bank
- [ ] Card reader's printed support number (in case of app update
      amnesia — see the [dry run](05-card-readers.md))
- [ ] Sales-tax rate card (if your state's tag prices are add-on
      style — see [permits](02-applications-and-permits.md))
- [ ] Price-signing pen (one that writes on cardstock *and* the
      tally sheet)

## The paperwork kit (a plastic sleeve in Tote 2)

- [ ] Sales-tax permit copy / registration number
- [ ] COI (if the event [required one](02-applications-and-permits.md))
- [ ] Booth-fee receipt copy
- [ ] Vendor contract copy
- [ ] One blank W-9
- [ ] Photo ID
- [ ] Event/load-in pass (if issued)
- [ ] One printed copy of [this checklist](packing-checklist.md)
      — that's this page, so you checked it twice

## The weather kit (season follows season)

- [ ] Plastic table cover / shower-curtain liner
- [ ] Ziplock bags for paper things ([tally sheets](06-honest-sales-tracking.md),
      permits, price cards)
- [ ] One towel (canopy poles weep)
- [ ] Dry socks; the [good shoes](08-slow-days-and-weather.md)
- [ ] Hand warmers (late season) / clip fan + battery (early season)
- [ ] Sunscreen / hat / rain shell (seller's PPE)
- [ ] Trash (yours, always): 2 bags

## The person kit (you are also at the event)

- [ ] Water (a real amount — 2 liters minimum)
- [ ] Lunch + snacks (the [feed-the-seller rule](08-slow-days-and-weather.md))
- [ ] Any meds you'd take on a normal workday, in their normal place
- [ ] Layers for the day's swing (mornings are cold; afternoons lie)
- [ ] Chapstick, wipes, one small first-aid pouch
- [ ] Band-aids in the *tool* pouch, not the first-aid pouch, if
      you're the kind of maker who knows why

## Load-in morning order (tape this inside Tote 1)

1. Canopy up, **weights on immediately**
2. Table in place, level check
3. Cloth on (hems even on the customer's side)
4. Crates and risers positioned
5. Signage up
6. Product on — [hero first](07-restock-math.md)
7. Money kit opened *last*, float counted once, then the [box
   lives on your side](04-pricing-and-making-change.md) of the
   table line

## Load-out night order (the reverse, plus the ritual)

1. Money box closed and counted ([the end-of-day count](04-pricing-and-making-change.md))
   *before* anything else moves
2. Product into totes (count as you go — this is the restock
   count, done in 2 minutes instead of 20 later)
3. Signage flat, cloth folded around the firm stuff
4. Table and risers into Tote 1
5. Canopy *last* to come down, weights stay bungee'd to the frame
6. One glance under the table — this finds the one thing every
   event almost takes from you
7. **[The review ritual](09-after-event-review.md) at the kitchen
   table** — tonight, not "this weekend"

## The two-rule summary of this whole page

1. **Two totes and one box** — if it doesn't fit the system, it
   doesn't come (the exception earns an exception by review, not
   by hope).
2. **Check the list the night before**, at the packing table, when
   errors are still free.
