# Glossary

Every word this handbook uses, defined like a friend would.
Cross-links hop to the chapter where the word earned its definition.

**All-in cost** — Booth fee + table rental + parking + permits +
lunch + everything. The number the [five-question event
test](01-finding-events.md) asks about, which is always bigger than
the advertised fee.

**Application** — The form an organizer uses to pick vendors and
plan the floor (power, tables, rows, jury). Matchmaking, mostly
([applications](02-applications-and-permits.md)).

**Bring-formula** — `rate/hour × hours × crowd factor × 1.3` =
what to pack. The one formula in the book you'll actually use
weekly ([restock math](07-restock-math.md)).

**Booth fee** — What you pay for the spot. Round-number shapes by
event type live in [chapter 1](01-finding-events.md); the
*bargaining* question is what's included (table? power? chair?).

**Back-stock bin** — The shelf under the table and the boxes in the
van: stock that exists but is not yet displayed. The showroom is
the table; the warehouse is the bin ([restock math](07-restock-math.md)).

**Buffer third (×1.3)** — The multiplier on the bring-formula that
absorbs the difference between last event and this one. Insurance,
not greed.

**Bungee** — The stretchy cord that attaches everything (weights,
totes, sidewalls, trash bags) to something portable. Might be the
actual most-used tool at markets.

**Cash dance (two-hop)** — Say the total aloud, cash flat on the
box, then count change into the customer's hand. The
[whole ritual](04-pricing-and-making-change.md), two hops, both out
loud.

**Cash float** — The $85 of small bills and coins you *lend* the
cash box at 10 a.m. so you can make change all day. Loaned, not
earned — subtracted at [close](04-pricing-and-making-change.md).

**Chargeback** — A card charge reversed by the customer's bank, at
the seller's expense, weeks later. Rare at markets, real as
weather. The defense is honest goods, honest totals said aloud,
and a happy receipt trail ([card readers](05-card-readers.md)).

**COI (certificate of insurance)** — The paper an organizer may ask
for to prove your vendor liability policy exists. Ask an insurance
professional — organizers tell you *what* they need; agents tell
you *what it costs* ([permits](02-applications-and-permits.md)).

**Crowd factor** — In the bring-formula, how much bigger/smaller
this event's traffic is than the one that taught you the rate.
Same market: 1.0. "Double last year," believed: 1.5, at most.

**Dead stock** — Product nobody touched by close. A data point
(pricing, placement, fit), not a guilt object ([tracking](06-honest-sales-tracking.md)).

**DBA / trade name** — A business display name different from the
legal name. Listed in the "describe your work" slot; the legal
name goes in the business-name slot
([permits](02-applications-and-permits.md)).

**EIN** — An Employer Identification Number: the IRS's free nine-
digit ID for a business, so your personal number isn't on every
form you hand an organizer ([permits](02-applications-and-permits.md)).

**Event scorecard** — The 1-5 verdict form you fill same-night, per
event, that steers next season's bookings
([scorecards](event-scorecards.md)).

**Floor** — The price of the three-layer cake below which you lose
money per item: materials + labor + show share. Tags sit *above*
the floor on purpose ([pricing](04-pricing-and-making-change.md)).

**Hero / filler / experiment** — The 70/25/5 stock split: your best
line, its helpers, and the one new thing ([restock math](07-restock-math.md)).

**Jury** — The screening some events do to pick a balanced room.
Applies early, photos real, statement short
([applications](02-applications-and-permits.md)).

**Load-in / load-out** — The 90 minutes of setup before open and
the 30-ish of teardown after. Two totes, one wagon, one order
([booth setup](03-booth-setup-on-a-budget.md), [checklist](packing-checklist.md)).

**LLC** — Limited liability company; a common small-business
structure. This book is LLC-*flavored* — it explains names and
forms, and never advises on whether to form one (that's a
professional conversation; see [what this book is not](index.md)).

**Margin of the day** — Net sales minus event costs: what the event
actually added ([tracking](06-honest-sales-tracking.md)). The
number that pays for upgrades.

**Offline mode** — The reader feature that queues card charges on
your device for later push, when there's no signal. Understands
its risk *before* the first gust day ([card readers](05-card-readers.md)).

**One-third rule** — When a third of a line is gone, restock that
line now. The visual math of a table that still looks alive.

**Over/short** — The recorded difference between what your sheet
says you took and what the box says. An honest log line, not a
scandal ([tracking](06-honest-sales-tracking.md)).

**Permit / seller's permit** — Formal state registration to sell
taxable goods; the name varies by state. One phone call to your
state's department of revenue settles most of it
([permits](02-applications-and-permits.md)).

**Price raft** — The three tiers (small/medium/large) that let a
browser read your whole table in one glance
([pricing](04-pricing-and-making-change.md)).

**Remit** — Hand the collected tax money to the state, on their
schedule. What happens after the envelope fills
([permits](02-applications-and-permits.md)).

**Restock** — Refilling a line mid-day from bin or van, before the
table looks bare. Also the *math chapter* that decides what you
pack ([restock math](07-restock-math.md)).

**RV / GSE note** — Vehicles and generators at events have their
own organizer rules; ask before you assume anything about parking,
power, or idling ([questions](01-finding-events.md)).

**Sales tax** — The buyer-paid portion, collected by you if your
state requires it, remitted on their schedule. Recorded as a
set-aside, not an opinion ([tracking](06-honest-sales-tracking.md)).

**Sell-through rate** — Units sold ÷ hours open. The rate that
feeds the bring-formula. Slow hours counted separately, for
festivals' sake ([restock math](07-restock-math.md)).

**Sidewall** — Zip-on canopy walls for windward weather; one or two
is plenty. The difference between "outdoor booth" and
"outdoor booth in spring rules" ([weather](08-slow-days-and-weather.md)).

**SKU** — Stock-keeping unit: one product-line/variant combination
on your restock list. Pencil-level bookkeeping, not software
([restock math](07-restock-math.md)).

**Switch scam** — The practiced "wait, I gave you bigger bills"
claim at busy tills. The [cash dance](04-pricing-and-making-change.md)
is the whole vaccine.

**Tally sheet** — The seven-column per-event record of every sale:
item, qty, price, cash, card, notes, and the totals footer
([tracking](06-honest-sales-tracking.md)).

**Vendor contract** — The agreement behind "I paid for a spot": fee,
refund-on-rain rule, load-in times, bump rules. Read before you
pay ([permits](02-applications-and-permits.md)).

**Waitlist** — The queue behind a full event. Not rejection — a
chance at a moving slot. Answer waitlist offers within a day or
two ([finding events](01-finding-events.md)).

**W-9** — The organizer's vendor-ID form: name, address, TIN. A
form, not a payment ([permits](02-applications-and-permits.md)).

**Wet go/no-go rule** — Your pre-written stop conditions for
storm-day calls, filled out in sunny weather
([scorecards](event-scorecards.md), [weather](08-slow-days-and-weather.md)).

**The Side-Hustle Reseller Handbook** — The companion book: selling
online (eBay/Amazon), listings, shipping, marketplace fees. This
book's in-person counterpart, published by the same folks
([relationship](index.md)).
