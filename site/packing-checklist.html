Packing Checklist

The night-before list, printable and checkable. Two totes, one wagon, one box — everything that leaves the house for an event, divided by job. The lists are designed around the two-tote load-in: Tote 1 is the structure (canopy, table, chair, weights), Tote 2 is the contents (cloth, product, signs, money kit), and both lids get a printed copy of this page.

Tote 1: Structure (goes up first)

  • ☐ Canopy, with its stakes/pegs bagged to the frame
  • ☐ Canopy weights — all four, bungee'd to the frame for carry
  • ☐ Sidewalls (1-2, weather season only)
  • ☐ Table (or table rental receipt if renting; check the organizer's table answer)
  • ☐ Tablecloth + skirt cloth
  • ☐ Chair (one is enough; you stand more than you sit)
  • ☐ Riser crates (2), the flat cart or suitcase shelving
  • ☐ Hand cart / wagon
  • ☐ Bungee cords (4), duct tape lap, sharpie

Tote 2: Contents (the product layer)

  • ☐ Stock by line, packed by the bring-formula, counted, in order of table placement
  • ☐ Van-box: the one back-stock box for hero lines (one per event, labeled)
  • ☐ Price cards / tags / the three-price raft signs
  • ☐ Banner / tabletop sign / business cards or tags
  • ☐ Display props in their labeled pouch (frames, stands, mirror)
  • ☐ Bags for customers (small paper or reusable, stocked)
  • ☐ Tissue / ribbon / small-gift wrap (if you offer it)

The money kit (the box with the lid card)

Everything money lives in one closable box with a card taped in the lid:

  • ☐ The lid card, printed: starting float breakdown ($40 ones / $25 fives / $20 tens / $10 coins = $85)
  • ☐ Cash box or money apron (make-change happens at the body)
  • ☐ Card reader, charged, with its cable
  • ☐ Phone, charged — and phone charger + battery bank
  • ☐ Card reader's printed support number (in case of app update amnesia — see the dry run)
  • ☐ Sales-tax rate card (if your state's tag prices are add-on style — see permits)
  • ☐ Price-signing pen (one that writes on cardstock and the tally sheet)

The paperwork kit (a plastic sleeve in Tote 2)

  • ☐ Sales-tax permit copy / registration number
  • ☐ COI (if the event required one)
  • ☐ Booth-fee receipt copy
  • ☐ Vendor contract copy
  • ☐ One blank W-9
  • ☐ Photo ID
  • ☐ Event/load-in pass (if issued)
  • ☐ One printed copy of this checklist — that's this page, so you checked it twice

The weather kit (season follows season)

  • ☐ Plastic table cover / shower-curtain liner
  • ☐ Ziplock bags for paper things (tally sheets, permits, price cards)
  • ☐ One towel (canopy poles weep)
  • ☐ Dry socks; the good shoes
  • ☐ Hand warmers (late season) / clip fan + battery (early season)
  • ☐ Sunscreen / hat / rain shell (seller's PPE)
  • ☐ Trash (yours, always): 2 bags

The person kit (you are also at the event)

  • ☐ Water (a real amount — 2 liters minimum)
  • ☐ Lunch + snacks (the feed-the-seller rule)
  • ☐ Any meds you'd take on a normal workday, in their normal place
  • ☐ Layers for the day's swing (mornings are cold; afternoons lie)
  • ☐ Chapstick, wipes, one small first-aid pouch
  • ☐ Band-aids in the tool pouch, not the first-aid pouch, if you're the kind of maker who knows why

Load-in morning order (tape this inside Tote 1)

  1. Canopy up, weights on immediately
  2. Table in place, level check
  3. Cloth on (hems even on the customer's side)
  4. Crates and risers positioned
  5. Signage up
  6. Product on — hero first
  7. Money kit opened last, float counted once, then the box lives on your side of the table line

Load-out night order (the reverse, plus the ritual)

  1. Money box closed and counted (the end-of-day count) before anything else moves
  2. Product into totes (count as you go — this is the restock count, done in 2 minutes instead of 20 later)
  3. Signage flat, cloth folded around the firm stuff
  4. Table and risers into Tote 1
  5. Canopy last to come down, weights stay bungee'd to the frame
  6. One glance under the table — this finds the one thing every event almost takes from you
  7. The review ritual at the kitchen table — tonight, not "this weekend"

The two-rule summary of this whole page

  1. Two totes and one box — if it doesn't fit the system, it doesn't come (the exception earns an exception by review, not by hope).
  2. Check the list the night before, at the packing table, when errors are still free.
